Rain Sounds Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

App: Rain Sounds for Android (com.advancedwholesale.rainsounds)

Developer: Odyssey

Contact: app.odyssey.us@gmail.com

Rain Sounds plays recorded rain sounds for sleep, relaxation and focus. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

Rain Sounds does not collect, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, sign-ins, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases. Nothing the app stores is sent to us or to anyone else.

Information we collect

None. The developer receives no data from the app.

Information stored only on your device

To work properly, the app keeps a small amount of information in its private storage on your phone:

  • Your settings and playback state: the rain sound you picked, the volume, whether rain is playing or paused, and when the sleep timer ends. This lets playback pick up where it left off if Android restarts the app.
  • A playback log: a small diagnostic log (limited to about 192 KB, with older entries removed automatically) that records when playback starts and stops and why. It can include the time of each event, your phone's maker and model, the Android version, the app version, whether battery optimization or power saving is on, the type and name of the audio output in use (for example "speaker" or the name of your Bluetooth headphones), and the reason Android last closed the app.

The app never uploads this information. The playback log stays on your device unless you choose to view it (long-press "Credits"), copy it, and send it to someone yourself. You can delete it with the "Clear" button in the log viewer, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include the app's settings and log in your own device backup, which is handled by Google under your Google account settings. We don't have access to those backups.

Internet and network use

All rain recordings are built into the app, so it works offline. The app declares the standard Internet permission because the framework it is built on includes it by default, but Rain Sounds does not use it to send or receive any data.

Permissions

Rain Sounds does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. It uses these standard Android permissions only to keep the rain playing:

  • Notifications and foreground service (media playback): to show a playback notification with controls and keep the rain going while the screen is off or you use other apps.
  • Wake lock: to keep playback from stopping while the phone sleeps.

Battery settings

Some phones stop background audio to save battery. The app checks whether battery optimization is on for Rain Sounds and may suggest turning it off. If you agree, it only opens Android's own settings screen; you make any change yourself, and the app doesn't change your settings for you.

Third parties

The app includes no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking code. Google Play may collect information when you download or update the app, under Google's own privacy policy.

Children

Rain Sounds is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the updated version at this address and update the effective date above.
